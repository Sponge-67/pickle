Pickle Privacy Policy

Home canning timer for Android · Last updated October 8, 2026

In short: Pickle does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

Pickle (“the app”) is made by Sponge-67. Questions about this policy: filamentfly@gmail.com.

Data the app stores

The app stores your canning sessions, batch log, pantry counts, seal-check results and settings (units, region, language and altitude range). This data is saved only on your device, inside the app’s private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

Location

Safe canning times depend on altitude. If you tap “Find it for me”, the app asks Android once for your approximate location (never precise location, never in the background) and looks up your altitude range in an elevation map stored inside the app. This lookup happens entirely on your phone.

The app keeps only the resulting altitude range, not your position, and never sends your location anywhere. You can type your altitude instead, or skip it: the app then assumes a high altitude, which is always safe. You can withdraw the location permission at any time in Android’s settings.

Purchases

The app is free. The one-time unlock for the pantry and batch log is optional and is processed entirely by Google Play, under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation that the unlock was bought; we never receive your name, email address or payment details. Everything that keeps your food safe works without paying.

What we share, and with whom

We do not share data with anyone, because the app does not send us any. The only third parties involved are Google, as the operator of Android and Google Play: Google Play handles the optional purchase, and Android may back up the app’s data (see below). Both happen under Google’s own privacy policy. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app’s data in your personal Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in Android’s settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries inside the app, clear everything in Android’s settings (Apps → Pickle → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from the device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

The app’s data is kept in Android’s private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is transmitted, so nothing can be intercepted on the way.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released, and the date at the top will change.
